# Festival photo

Place your original, licensed photo here as `festival_photo.jpg`.
Both media cards load that path automatically. Re-run `flutter pub get` and
restart the app after adding it to the asset bundle.

Prefer a portrait photo (roughly 4:5 for the spotted card, 2:3 for the moment
preview), showing someone returning a cup or picking up litter at a festival.
The cards crop with BoxFit.cover. An illustrated, labeled placeholder appears
when the file is absent or unreadable. Figma screenshots are references only;
they are not cropped or repurposed as original photography.

A portrait MP4 around 15 seconds long will be needed for Phase 2, when a video
player and asset registration will be added. No video player is included yet.
